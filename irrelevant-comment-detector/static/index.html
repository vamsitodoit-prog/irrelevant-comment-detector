How the detector decides

Relevance blends TF‑IDF cosine similarity between the comment and the post (title counted twice) with keyword overlap, after removing stop‑words and stemming. Spam adds up signals: links, contact handles, phone numbers, self‑promotion phrases, money bait, all caps and repeated characters. The Python backend also runs a trained logistic‑regression classifier and combines it with these rules. Low‑effort catches short filler such as "nice video" or emoji‑only replies.
